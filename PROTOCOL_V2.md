# V2 — correction after Neon audit, before inspecting V1 quick.json

V1 is preserved. Its successful local execution is known, but its numerical outcomes have not been inspected here. Do not pool V1 and V2.

1. Replace literal identity-copy questions with paired nonlexical properties. Broad and middle questions still name categories. All three levels are forced-choice; measure clean confidence and accuracy. The small set is a pilot, not a clean separation of semantic levels from task difficulty.
2. Compare the original item-only cut with a single-layer cut across all positions from the first item token through the answer prefix. At a decoder layer boundary with cache disabled, later positions cannot bypass the full suffix cut through an unmodified copy at that boundary. However rounding is not erasure and surviving information can still be decoded; full-suffix changes also perturb question representations and therefore are not selective semantic ablations. Compare the scope interaction, not raw accuracies alone.
3. Donor transplant: same question and options, opposite-answer donor. Only count aligned token sequences with both source and donor clean correct. Report eligibility and flip rate separately by scope, layer and grain. Full suffix donor is a positive plumbing control, not evidence of a concept-specific causal pathway. Low item-only flip rate means the original site is insufficient to interpret null effects.
4. J vocabulary bias: equal-rank (8) category, leaf, independent and balanced J subspaces, plus random rank-8 and within-balanced rotated basis. Report actual single-token vocabulary. Balanced basis uses four principal gradient directions from each vocabulary before orthonormalization; this balances the construction, not semantic content. Do not call the independent vocabulary semantically neutral. Primary numeric comparisons are native vs rotated full-space. 
5. Equal per-token relative L2 dose across nonzero quantization-error directions: 0.025, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2. Use bits 2 and 4 to generate directions, then rescale; the final state is NOT strictly quantized. V1 remains the raw quantization reference. Report raw dose and realized dose after dtype conversion. Match each direction to isotropic random perturbation and for low-rank arms also in-span random perturbation. Zero error directions stay zero. No selection on held-out answers.
6. Compare excess signed margin loss versus random (and in-span random), fine minus broad, by item, seed, scope, layer and dose. Check clean-correct subset and confidence; do not claim hierarchy from aggregate accuracy. Summarizer prints descriptive cells and transplant gates; paired confidence-matched inference and independent family expansion remain follow-ups. No arbitrary pass threshold from a pilot.
7. No claims of biological ephaptic coupling or novel quantization. No joint claim with Claude until checkpoint and tasks match. Claude currently defaults to Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct; V2 retains user's already downloaded Qwen3-1.7B unless --model overrides.
